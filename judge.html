Paste the role's requirements, or a whole job description. A TypeSafe Jev model checks them against the recorded work history and answers with probabilities instead of opinions. Every judgment it made is shown.

How the answer is reached

The model is asked four questions about the pasted text and the record: how directly the record shows this responsibility (0-3), whether the requirement names something the record never mentions, whether the stated scale is larger than anything in the record, and whether the role is about running people or money. The verdict is decided in code from those four numbers, and every threshold is visible in the source.

The record is a fixed file: three roles, the systems, the numbers, and a written list of what is not evidenced. Nothing about the visitor is stored, and no question text is kept after the request.
